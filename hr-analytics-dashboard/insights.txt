HR ANALYTICS DASHBOARD - KEY INSIGHTS

1. Research & Development experienced the highest employee attrition, followed by Sales and Human Resources.

2. Employees working overtime showed a higher likelihood of leaving the organisation compared to employees not working overtime.

3. Average salary levels varied across departments, reflecting differences in job functions and responsibilities.

4. The workforce was primarily concentrated within the mid-career age range, indicating a relatively mature employee base.

5. Job satisfaction levels were generally moderate, although differences existed across employee groups.

6. The overall attrition rate highlights the importance of retention initiatives and workforce planning strategies.
